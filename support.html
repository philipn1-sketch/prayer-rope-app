Help and support

Your rule

Setup asks how you pray: language, prayers, rope size, prostrations and how often. Change any of it with Edit under Your rule. A rule can have several prayers in order, each for a number of ropes, once a day or morning and evening. Choose "My own prayer" to use your own words.

Praying

Tap Pray and tap anywhere on the dark screen for each knot. The phone taps back, more firmly at marker beads and at the end of each rope. You can undo a tap, keep praying past your rule, or, if you prayed with your own rope, mark the rule as kept with one tap.

Streak and calendar

Your streak counts days in a row keeping your whole rule, or days you prayed at all (choose in Settings), or you can turn it off. Tap any day on the calendar to log or edit it. The church calendar shows feasts and fasts on the Old or New Calendar; follow your parish calendar where it differs.

Prayer groups

Tap the people icon and sign in with Apple or Google. Create a group and tap Invite people to send its 6-character code, or Join with a code someone sent you. Members see who has prayed today and can share prayer requests; tap I prayed to let someone know you prayed for them. Press and hold a request to report it or block its author.

Widgets, reminders and backup

Widgets, history older than 30 days and backup are part of Prayer Rope Pro, $1.99 a month or $14.99 a year with a free week on the yearly plan. Get it, restore it or manage it in Settings. Add the Prayer Rope widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen. Turn on morning and evening reminders in Settings. Export your history as a CSV file in Settings to keep a backup, and import it on a new phone.

Deleting your account

Tap the people icon, then Delete my account. This removes everything stored for prayer groups. Your prayer history on your phone isn't affected.
