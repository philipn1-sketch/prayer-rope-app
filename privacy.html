Prayer Rope

Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

Prayer Rope is made by QuickQuote Group PTY LTD, an Australian company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app stores, where, and who can see it. In short: your prayer life stays on your phone, nothing is sold, and there are no ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your prayer rule, prayer counts, prostrations, notes, streak, reminders and settings are stored only on your device (and in your own iPhone backups). We never receive them. If you export a backup, the file goes wherever you choose to save it.

Prayer groups

Prayer groups are optional. If you use them, you sign in with Apple or Google, and we store the following with Google Firebase, our service provider:

  • Account details: an account identifier and the email address Apple or Google gives us (with Sign in with Apple this can be a private relay address). Used only to sign you in.
  • Your name: the first name you enter, shown to people in your groups.
  • Groups: the groups you create or join.
  • "Prayed today": the date of the last day you prayed, if you choose to share it. You can turn this off at any time.
  • Prayer requests: requests you share, and which members tapped "I prayed".
  • Safety: people you block, and reports you make (the reported request, its author and the group), so we can review them.

Only members of a group can see that group, its members and its prayer requests. Data is stored on Google Cloud servers in the United States and protected by access rules so that people can only read groups they belong to.

Purchases

Prayer Rope Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment and tells the app whether Pro is active. We don't receive your name, email or payment details.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your information, show ads, use analytics, or track you across other apps and websites. We use your information only to run the prayer groups.

Keeping and deleting your information

Group information is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (people icon, then Delete my account). This removes your profile, your membership of every group, your prayer requests, and any groups you created, and signs you out of Apple or Google for this app. Reports may be kept for up to 12 months for safety reasons.

Children

Prayer groups are not intended for children under 13. Parents can contact us to have a child's account deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or complain about how we handle it, by emailing us. If you're in Australia you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner; if you're in the EU or UK, your local data protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
