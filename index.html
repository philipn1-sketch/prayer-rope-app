Prayer Rope

Keep your daily prayer rule with the prayer rope, and pray together with family and friends.

Build your own rule with the Jesus Prayer and other prayers, in six languages. Tap for each knot and the phone taps back. Keep a gentle streak, follow the Old or New church calendar with its feasts and fasts, and use widgets and reminders. Small private prayer groups show who has prayed today and let you share prayer requests.
